# Guide Starter

The Perry Group AI class, Part 2. This repo becomes your niche guide.

## How to use it

Step 1: Click the green Use this template button (top right), then Create a new repository. Name it for your guide, all lowercase with dashes: fsbo-guide, divorce-guide, downsizing-guide.

Step 2: In your new repo go to Settings, then Pages. Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save. That turns on your live site.

Step 3: Your live page will be at YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO-NAME after it builds (a minute or two).

Step 4: Open your repo in Chrome, open Claude in Chrome, and paste the build prompt below with your five answers filled in.

## The build prompt (copy everything between the two lines)

---

I'm a real estate agent with The Perry Group | Real Broker LLC in Utah County. Use the perry-group-style skill for everything you build and write for me.

We are turning this repo into my niche guide page. The index.html in this repo is a plain starter. Replace its placeholder content with my guide. Keep it one single index.html file with all CSS inside it, ready for GitHub Pages.

MY FIVE ANSWERS:

Answer 1, who I'm trying to help: [paste your answer]

Answer 2, what I know about them that most agents don't: [paste your answer]

Answer 3, the free thing I'm giving them: [paste your answer]

Answer 4, what I want them to do next: [paste your answer]

Answer 5, about me (name, cell, two sentences about me): [paste your answer]

RULES FOR THIS BUILD:

Before writing anything, show me a short section outline and wait for my approval. Ask me questions at that stage if my answers above leave gaps.

Never fill a gap with generic advice, and never invent numbers, statistics, or local facts. If a section needs a number or local detail I have not given you, ask me for it.

Every section must contain at least one thing a Google search cannot return: my numbers, my town, my experience.

The page ends with one clear next step for the reader, built from answer 4, as a text-me link that opens a prewritten text to my cell.

My logos, headshot, and photos are in a folder on my desktop. Tell me which files to upload and where they go.

Start with the outline.

---

## If something breaks

404 but "site is live": the homepage must be named exactly index.html, lowercase, at the top level. Wait 2 to 3 minutes after a change, then hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R).

Page not updating: check the repo home page for an orange dot (still building) or green check (done) next to the latest commit.

Guide sounds generic: tell Claude "this fails the thirty second test, ask me for my specifics instead."
